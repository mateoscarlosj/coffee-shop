Our Story

A neighborhood cafe with a serious love for coffee.

Tala started in 2019 as a small corner counter with one espresso machine and a simple idea: great coffee should feel personal. Today, we remain independent, sourcing directly from growers in Benguet, Sagada, and Mt. Apo.

Every cup is dialed in by hand, every pastry is baked in-house, and every guest is welcome to stay as long as they like.
